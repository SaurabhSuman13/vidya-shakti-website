Ways your support could help

01 · Learning materials

More hands-on early learning

Sample planning cost: ₹800 per learning kit

Could support: picture books, pencils, counting aids, flashcards and shared classroom activity kits.

Potential benefit: More opportunities to practise language, numbers and foundational concepts through guided activities.

02 · Digital literacy

More practice with computers

Sample planning cost: ₹1,500 per device service pack

Could support: keyboard or mouse replacements, headphones, offline learning content or basic maintenance.

Potential benefit: More functional practice opportunities for typing, basic computer use and digital learning tasks.

03 · Classroom space

A more usable classroom

Sample planning cost: ₹2,500 per equipment unit

Could support: benches, mats, storage, lighting improvements or essential classroom equipment.

Potential benefit: Address a specific classroom barrier identified by teachers and management.

04 · Community engagement

Help families learn about programmes

Sample planning cost: ₹2,500 per outreach activity

Could support: parent meetings, printed programme information and local outreach.

Potential benefit: Families can better understand available learning programmes and how to enquire or enrol.

05 · Teacher support

More structured classroom activities

Sample planning cost: ₹3,000 per resource set

Could support: lesson-planning resources, teaching aids, activity templates or skills-sharing materials.

Potential benefit: Help teachers prepare repeatable activities matched to students' learning levels.

06 · Creative learning

More room to create and collaborate

Sample planning cost: ₹1,000 per activity kit

Could support: art supplies, sports equipment, music materials or group-project resources.

Potential benefit: More structured opportunities for creativity, teamwork and self-expression.

Beyond supplies

Contributions can take different forms

💚 Financial contributions

Allocate an approved budget to a clearly defined need.

  • Books, stationery and learning kits
  • Computer maintenance and digital resources
  • Benches and basic classroom equipment

🧰 In-kind resources

Provide useful goods or services after confirming suitability.

  • Working computers and accessories
  • Age-appropriate books and teaching aids
  • Repairs, printing or connectivity support

👩‍🏫 Time and expertise

Contribute skills in coordination with the organisation.

  • Digital-skills sessions
  • Teacher resource preparation
  • Career exposure and activity workshops

🤝 Community partnerships

Help strengthen the connection between families and learning programmes.

  • Parent information meetings
  • Local awareness activities
  • Volunteering and programme referrals

Responsible impact tracking

From support to evidence

Confirm the need

Discuss priorities with educators and management before accepting or allocating resources.

Record the input

Document approved resources, costs, dates and intended activity.

Track the output

Record what was delivered or conducted, such as kits distributed or sessions held.

Review the outcome

Use attendance, teacher feedback and suitable learning checks; report limitations honestly.
