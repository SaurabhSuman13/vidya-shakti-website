About the organisation

Learning is the beginning of possibility.

Vidya Shakti Niwas works in Bilaipada and provides free early education for children in playschool, nursery, LKG and UKG. The organisation also runs a computer training centre and offers music and dance activities.

This website is designed to help the organisation explain its work, share verified programme information, and make it easier for community members, volunteers and potential supporters to get in touch.

About this website: Please ask the organisation to review and approve all public-facing information before the site is shared widely.

Early education

A foundation for lifelong learning

Playschool, nursery, LKG and UKG provide a setting for children to begin developing foundational language and classroom-learning skills.

Computer literacy

Opening a door to digital skills

The centre reports weekly computer classes focused on basic computer skills. These sessions offer a starting point for learners to become familiar with digital tools.

Guidance that supports participation

Teachers help organise learning activities and guide children through classroom lessons. Their perspective can help explain what supports children and what resources are still needed.
